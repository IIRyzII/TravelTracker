ORBIT

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

ORBIT is run by IIRyzII, based in England ("we", "us"). We decide how your personal data is used, which makes us its "controller" under UK data protection law. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it and the choices you have. Questions or requests: ryleycodes@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

WhatWhyLegal basis

"Contract" means we need the data to provide ORBIT to you. "Legitimate interests" means we use it to keep ORBIT secure, working and affordable, in ways you'd reasonably expect.

Who can see your data

  • Anyone you give your share code or invite link to. Using your code makes them your friend straight away, with no approval step. Friends can see your display name, the countries you've visited and how many countries you've been to. They can't see your email, cities, wishlist or trips. Only share your code with people you're happy to see your map. You can remove a friend, or get a new code, at any time.
  • People on a trip can see that trip and the display names of everyone on it.
  • We never show your email address to other users. Nobody else can see your data. We don't sell it, we don't show ads and we don't use advertising or analytics trackers.

Services we use

These companies process data for us, only to provide ORBIT:

  • Render hosts ORBIT. Our database is stored in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Resend sends our emails: password resets and inactivity warnings. It receives your email address and the message.
  • Open-Meteo receives the place names you type into search, to find cities. Our server sends the search, so Open-Meteo doesn't receive your IP address or who you are.
  • Google Maps: if live place results are switched on, Google receives the destination names you search in the Trip planner. As with Open-Meteo, our server makes the request, so Google doesn't learn who you are.

International transfers

Render and Resend are US companies and Google operates worldwide, so some data may be accessed from, or sent to, countries outside the UK. When that happens we rely on the safeguards in each provider's data processing terms, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Cookies and storage on your device

  • One cookie, orbit_session, keeps you signed in.
  • Your browser remembers that you've signed in on this device before, so we show "Sign in" first.
  • If you open a friend's invite link, their code is kept until you finish signing up.
  • A copy of ORBIT's app files is saved so it loads quickly and works offline.

These are needed to provide the features you use, so we don't show a cookie banner. We don't use analytics or advertising cookies.

How long we keep it

  • While you use ORBIT, we keep your data.
  • If you don't use ORBIT for 3 months, we delete your account and all its data. We email you a week beforehand. Signing in before then keeps your account.
  • When you delete your account (Account → Delete account), it's removed from our database straight away and from our backups within 7 days. Trips you own are deleted for everyone on them. Your votes and places on other people's trips are removed.
  • Server logs are kept for up to 30 days.

How we protect it

Passwords are stored only as secure hashes, never in readable form. All traffic uses HTTPS. Every request is checked so you can only reach your own data and the trips you're on. Changing your password signs you out on every other device.

Your rights

  • Access and portability: Account → Download my data gives you everything as a file.
  • Correction: you can edit your name, lists and trips at any time.
  • Deletion: Account → Delete account.
  • Objection and restriction: email us to object to, or ask us to limit, how we use your data.

We'll reply to requests within one month. We don't make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Children

You must be 13 or over to use ORBIT. If we find out that someone under 13 has an account, we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top. If the change is significant, we'll tell you in the app or by email first.

Cookies and storage

ORBIT uses one essential cookie, orbit_session, to keep you signed in. It's strictly necessary, so there's no cookie banner. Your browser also stores a note that you've visited before (to show "Sign in" first) and, briefly, a friend's invite code while you sign up. We don't use analytics or advertising cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete your account (Account → Delete account) it is removed from our live database straight away and from backups within [7] days.

  • Deletion — Account → Delete account.
